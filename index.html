Adithya Sathyanarayana

Ph.D. Candidate · Department of Mathematics · Michigan State University

Email GitHub ORCID CV

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Michigan State University, working with Prof. Georgios Pappas. I completed a B.S.–M.S. dual degree in mathematics at IISER Tirupati in 2022, where I worked with Prof. D. S. Nagaraj and wrote my master’s thesis on class field theory.

I am broadly interested in arithmetic geometry and representation theory, specifically finite group actions on algebraic varieties and the Galois module structure of their cohomology groups. Recently I have been working on Deligne–Lusztig varieties; see my preprint under preparation and the research page.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy Carnatic and Hindustani classical music, playing the bansuri (Indian bamboo flute), badminton, literature, and social justice advocacy.

I am on the academic job market for positions starting Fall 2027.

Research interests

  • Galois module structure and Deligne–Lusztig varieties
  • Computational aspects of Drinfeld modules
  • Arithmetic statistics of class groups of number fields
