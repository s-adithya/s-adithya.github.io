Research

My research focuses on problems in arithmetic geometry and representation theory. My current interests are:

  • Galois module structure: how Galois groups act on the cohomology of algebraic varieties
  • Deligne–Lusztig varieties: their explicit geometry and Galois module structure
  • Computational aspects of Drinfeld modules
  • Arithmetic statistics of class groups of number fields

Arithmetic statistics of p-torsion in class groups of quartic number fields

2026–present · Arithmetic Geometry at UNT 2, University of North Texas · Sameera Vemulapalli’s project group · Repository

Who bears the congestion price?

Summer 2026 · Erdős Institute Data Science Boot Camp · Repository

On January 5, 2025, New York became the first US city to charge vehicles for entering a defined zone: the Manhattan Central Business District south of 60th Street. Using public TLC trip records (about 37M Yellow taxi trips and about 202M Uber/Lyft trips, February–June 2024 compared with the same months of 2025), we ask who bears this fee and whether it reduced trips.

  • The burden is uneven, and the finding is stable. A flat per-trip fee is a much larger share of short, low-cost trips, which are concentrated in the dense Manhattan core, than of long airport trips. The zone ranking stays stable across the tested denominator floors, giving a reusable, reproducible burden metric (DS_z).
  • The volume models show suggestive negative patterns. Three regression-with-controls designs compare burden, exposure, and vehicle groups. A 2023→2024 no-fee placebo and sensitivity checks limit a direct fee-caused interpretation.
